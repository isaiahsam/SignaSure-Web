# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 2025**

## Introduction

Welcome to SignaSure. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Documents and Images
- **Document Scans**: When you use SignaSure to scan or upload documents, we process the images and extracted text locally on your device.
- **Document Analysis**: We send document text to third-party AI services (Google Gemini API) for analysis purposes.
- **Local Storage**: All scanned documents and analysis results are stored locally on your device using SQLite database.

### Personal Information
- **Username**: We collect an optional username for personalization purposes.
- **Device Information**: We may collect basic device information for app functionality and debugging purposes.

### Authentication Data
- We do not currently collect authentication credentials as the app operates in local mode.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:
- Analyze legal documents and contracts for potential risks and important clauses
- Provide personalized recommendations and insights
- Improve our document analysis capabilities
- Maintain and improve app functionality
- Provide customer support

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage
- All your documents, scans, and analysis results are stored locally on your device
- We use SQLite database with encryption to protect your data
- Data remains on your device unless you choose to delete it

### Third-Party Services
- We use Google Gemini API for AI-powered document analysis
- Document text (not images) is sent to Google's servers for processing
- We recommend reviewing Google's Privacy Policy for information about their data practices

## Data Sharing

We do **NOT**:
- Sell your personal information to third parties
- Share your documents with unauthorized parties
- Use your data for marketing purposes without consent

We **MAY** share data:
- With your explicit consent
- To comply with legal obligations
- With service providers who assist in app functionality (e.g., Google Gemini API)

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data stored in the app
- Delete your documents and data at any time through the app
- Opt out of data collection by not using certain features
- Request information about how your data is processed

## Data Retention

- Documents and analysis results are retained on your device until you choose to delete them
- You can delete individual documents or clear all data through the app settings
- Uninstalling the app will remove all locally stored data

## Children's Privacy

SignaSure is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Posting the new Privacy Policy in the app
- Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
